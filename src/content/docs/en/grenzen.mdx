## What this site is

A **readable, recalculated rendering of the BSI's Grundschutz++ catalog**. It answers four questions (the catalog pages are in German):

- **What is in it?** All requirements, sorted by [practice](/grundschutzpp/), [target object](/zielobjekte/) and [document](/dokumente/), with full-text search and an [Excel export](/export/).
- **What has changed?** The [change log](/aenderungen/) records every new BSI version. You can subscribe to it via RSS.
- **What is wrong or unclear?** The [findings](/befunde/) (German) list contradictions and gaps in the catalog, with evidence and classification.
- **Where did it go?** The [comparison pages](/vergleich/) show where building blocks of the old Kompendium went in the new catalog, according to the BSI mapping.

Every number on the site is checked in tests against the BSI's raw catalog. The catalog and the mappings always come from the BSI, with source, version and license.

## Where the site stops

- **It is not an offering of the BSI** and has no connection to the Federal Office. The official original always governs.
- **It does not advise on individual cases.** It does not say whether anyone is affected, fulfills or must implement something. The users or their advisors decide that.
- **It does not invent mappings.** There are no mappings of its own to other frameworks. Only mappings the BSI itself publishes are shown, with their status.
- **It does not replace the standard.** BSI Standard 300-1 is summarized, not reproduced. The BSI's [terms of use](https://www.bsi.bund.de/DE/Service/Nutzungsbedingungen/Nutzungsbedingungen_node.html) apply (German).

More on who makes the site and how it stays current: [About this site](/en/ueber/).

## What is missing for you?

The site is built around what is needed. If something is missing, say so:

- [**Report an idea or need**](https://github.com/grundschutz-docs/docs/issues/new?template=idea_need.yml): What would you need, and for which task?
- [**Report an error in the catalog**](https://github.com/grundschutz-docs/docs/issues/new?template=catalog_finding.yml): A contradiction, a gap or a wrong value in the BSI catalog.
- [**Report a problem with the site**](https://github.com/grundschutz-docs/docs/issues/new?template=site_bug.yml): A wrong number, a dead link or a display problem.

The same goes for pilot partners: if you are introducing Grundschutz++ and would like to work with part of this, you are welcome.
