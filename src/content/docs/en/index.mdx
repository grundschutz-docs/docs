It applies regardless of where your company is headquartered: what
matters is whether you're established in Germany or serve customers
here (§§ 59, 60 BSIG). IT-Grundschutz isn't automatically mandatory for
everyone this law touches — [BSI's own FAQ](https://www.bsi.bund.de/DE/Themen/Regulierte-Wirtschaft/NIS-2-regulierte-Unternehmen/NIS-2-FAQ/NIS-2-FAQ-allgemein/FAQ-zu-NIS-2.html)
is explicit about that. But an existing Grundschutz-based ISMS is, in
BSI's own words, "a valuable foundation" for meeting it.

## What Grundschutz++ is

Germany's Federal Office for Information Security (BSI) is replacing
its longstanding IT-Grundschutz framework with **Grundschutz++**: a
leaner, machine-readable catalog (in [OSCAL](https://pages.nist.gov/OSCAL/))
rather than the old PDF-based Kompendium, phased in over several years.
This site is an **independent, unofficial** rendering of it — not a
BSI product — built to actually search and read: full-text search, one
permalink per requirement, a mapping back to the old Kompendium control
it replaces.
