## What Grundschutz++ is

Germany's Federal Office for Information Security (BSI) is replacing its
longstanding IT-Grundschutz framework with **Grundschutz++**: a leaner,
machine-readable catalog of security requirements (in [OSCAL](https://pages.nist.gov/OSCAL/),
a NIST-originated standard) rather than the old PDF-based
Kompendium. It's being phased in over several years, alongside the
Kompendium it eventually replaces.

This site is an **independent, unofficial** rendering of that catalog —
not a BSI product. It takes the 10,000-line source file BSI publishes
for tooling and SSP-generation and turns it into something you can
actually search and read: full-text search, one permalink per
requirement, and a mapping back to the old Kompendium control it
replaces. More on why this project exists under
[About this site](/ueber/) (German for now — see below).

It applies regardless of where your company is headquartered: what
matters is whether you're established in Germany or serve customers
here (§§ 59, 60 BSIG). IT-Grundschutz isn't automatically mandatory for
everyone this law touches — [BSI's own FAQ](https://www.bsi.bund.de/DE/Themen/Regulierte-Wirtschaft/NIS-2-regulierte-Unternehmen/NIS-2-FAQ/NIS-2-FAQ-allgemein/FAQ-zu-NIS-2.html)
is explicit about that. But an existing Grundschutz-based ISMS is, in
BSI's own words, "a valuable foundation" for meeting it.

## What's in English here — and what isn't

Right now, this overview page is the only thing translated. The
requirement catalog itself, the role-specific guides (for executives,
security leads, and engineering), the old↔new mapping, and the findings
register are all still German-only — translating those is a separate,
much bigger task this project hasn't tackled yet.

Follow a link past this page and you'll land on the German version;
the site will tell you plainly when that happens, rather than quietly
showing you a stub. If you read German, or have a colleague who does,
the catalog starts at [/grundschutzpp/](/grundschutzpp/), the
role guides at [/rollen/](/rollen/geschaeftsfuehrung/), and the
old↔new mapping at [/vergleich/](/vergleich/).
